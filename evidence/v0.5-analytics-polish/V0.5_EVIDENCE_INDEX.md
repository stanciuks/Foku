## Privacy and design principle

Foku remains local-first and deterministic.

The analytics in this milestone are based on:
- session timing,
- completion,
- self-check rating,
- intention/subject tags,
- reflection note presence.

The app does not inspect the screen, browser history, messages, files, keystrokes, or websites.
